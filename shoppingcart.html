ABOUT THE PROJECT

A boutique store
built from scratch.

Yasmine Shopping Cart is a Laravel-based full-stack e-commerce application designed around a boutique shopping experience for hijabs and accessories.

Customers can browse products, view individual product details, add items to their cart, and complete the checkout process. The application also includes an administrative dashboard for managing products and maintaining the online store.
