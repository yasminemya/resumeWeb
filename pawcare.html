ABOUT THE PROJECT

Built for pet owners, designed with care.

PawCare is a full-stack web application that allows pet owners to discover and book services such as grooming, veterinary care, and pet sitting.

The application includes customer and provider dashboards, authentication, booking functionality, and a review system.
